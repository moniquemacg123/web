About Me

Strategy meets
operations.

I'm a B2B marketing leader focused on using technology to solve problems, improve how teams work, and drive measurable results. Across startups, enterprise technology companies, and Walmart, I've built digital marketing functions, led teams, developed MarTech and web strategies, improved processes, and connected marketing programs to measurable business results.

More recently, that curiosity has pulled me deeply into AI. I've helped put generative AI into practice inside enterprise marketing platforms, working through everything from use-case evaluation and legal approval to governance and user adoption. I also build AI agents hands-on using Claude Code, the Anthropic API, Cassidy AI, and n8n.

What interests me most about AI isn't the hype, it's figuring out where it can genuinely improve how people work. I like experimenting with new tools, understanding how they work, and turning the useful ones into practical workflows that save time, improve decisions, or solve problems in new ways.
